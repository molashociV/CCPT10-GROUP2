CBDRRM encourages people to work together to identify risks, prepare for disasters, respond safely, and recover as a community.

What is CBDRRM?

Community-Based Disaster Risk Reduction and Management involves the community in disaster preparedness, response, and recovery. Everyone has a role in keeping the community safe.

How to Prepare

  • Reduce hazards around your home and community.
  • Listen to official warnings and announcements.
  • Know evacuation routes and safe places.
  • Prepare a go-bag with water, food, flashlight, first-aid supplies, and important documents.
  • Join disaster drills and practice emergency plans.
  • Help children, older adults, and people who need assistance.
  • Protect the environment and keep waterways clean.

Community Action

Identify risks: Know possible floods, landslides, earthquakes, and fires.

Make a plan: Create a family and community evacuation plan.

Share information: Teach others about warnings and safe routes.

Recover together: Support affected families after a disaster.

Why Preparedness Matters

Being prepared can help people stay calm, make safer decisions, and protect lives during disasters.

  • Reduces possible risks and damage.
  • Helps families respond quickly.
  • Strengthens cooperation among neighbors.
  • Helps communities recover faster and safely.

Before, During, and After a Disaster

Before: Prepare supplies, learn evacuation routes, and join drills.

During: Stay calm, listen to authorities, and move to a safe place when instructed.

After: Check on others, follow safety instructions, and help the community recover.

Remember

Disaster preparedness is not only the responsibility of authorities. Every person can help make the community safer and more resilient.

Common Community Hazards

  • Floods
  • Landslides
  • Earthquakes
  • Fires

Knowing the hazards in your area helps the community prepare appropriate safety plans.

Emergency Go-Bag

A basic go-bag can include:

  • Drinking water and food
  • Flashlight
  • First-aid supplies
  • Important documents
  • Other essential personal items

Work as One Community

Prepared communities communicate, cooperate, and support one another before, during, and after disasters.

Every person can help. Every action matters.
