My Choice, My Responsibility

Digital reflection and community pledge

My digital reflection

I learned that doing what is right is not always easy. Moral courage means choosing what is right even when others may disagree.

I should think carefully before making decisions because my actions can affect other people. Integrity means staying honest and responsible even when no one is watching.

As a member of my community, I can show these values by respecting others, helping people, and doing my part to make a positive difference.

Core values

Moral courage

I will stand for what is right even when it is difficult.

Responsible decision-making

I will think before I act and consider how my decisions affect others.

Integrity

I will be honest, responsible, and true to my values.

Service

I will help others and use my abilities to make a positive difference.

Community pledge

I pledge to make responsible decisions, be honest in my words and actions, and stand for what is right. I will respect others, help my community, and use my choices to make a positive difference every day.

Small choices can create a big difference.
